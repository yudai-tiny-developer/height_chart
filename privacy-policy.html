Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

No Data Collection

Height Chart Generator does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any user data. The application runs locally in your browser.

No File Uploads

Files that you select, drag, or drop into the application are processed only on your device. They are not uploaded to any server, cloud service, analytics service, or third party.

Local Processing

Image files and CSV content are used only to generate the height chart in your browser. The generated chart can be downloaded by you, and the application does not send it anywhere.

Cookies and Analytics

This application does not use cookies, tracking technologies, or analytics scripts.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be included with the application.
